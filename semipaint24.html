Hardware: Motorola MC6847 VDG + MC6883 SAM
Resolution: 64×192 pixels / 32×192 cells (2px × 1px per cell)

Byte format: D7=SG flag · D6–D4=color · D3=left · D2=right · D1D0=don't care
